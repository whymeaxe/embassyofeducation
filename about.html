Brand clarification

“Embassy of Education” is a private business name.

We are not an embassy, consulate, government body or official visa-decision authority. We provide independent advisory and education consultancy services.

Our purpose

Turn visa confusion into structured case clarity.

A refusal often leaves applicants with a legal code but little personalised understanding of what to review. Our work is to organise the available facts, identify visible decision-risk areas and help the applicant make an informed, truthful next decision.

We are particularly focused on B1/B2 visitor and business cases, F-1 student cases, repeat refusals, DS-160 inconsistencies, family applications and high-risk first-time profiles.

What we ask from clients

Good strategy depends on complete and accurate information.

  • Disclose all previous applications and refusals
  • Provide genuine documents only
  • Review every form before submission
  • Inform us of material changes promptly
  • Attend preparation sessions on time
  • Do not rely on another applicant’s script
  • Follow official government instructions
